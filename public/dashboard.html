GETTING THE BEST ANALYSIS

Atlas reads your body position frame by frame, so it works best on clear footage of just you — filmed straight on or from a clean side angle, with your whole body in frame. Even 5–10 seconds is plenty.

Game footage is welcome, but worth knowing: when other players cross in front of you or the camera pans to follow the play, our vision model can lose track of which body is yours. Scores from game clips are less reliable, and we can't return an annotated overlay video for them.

If you can grab a quick clip of yourself skating on your own before or after a session, that will always give you a sharper report than a full game.
